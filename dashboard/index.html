How to read this board

Implied value is the expected value of a launch: the gross value if a token launches (the fitted size curve at the protocol's TVL, times its category's factor, times one more week of its 7-day change, times an age factor) times the launch odds — the chance of a token within 12 months, from a Weibull survival model fitted on how long the snapshot's 416 token-recorded protocols ran before recording one. P25–P75 is the range over 200 bootstrap refits, widened by the model's held-out error. Verdict compares the estimated value per farmer (10% of the expected value, shared by an estimated farmer count) with what the farm plan costs: FARM clears three times the cost, WATCH is the rest — there is no skip, because a launch candidate is always worth watching. The model is refitted in this browser from the sealed snapshot, and the integrity strip above shows the refit agreeing with board.json row by row.

Assumptions, stated as ones: the farmer count (25 × √TVL in $M, clamped to 100–5,000) and the 10% farmer share are estimates — the snapshot records neither. Gas is a per-chain table ($8 Ethereum, $5 Bitcoin, $2 other, $1 the rest). The T-bill benchmark is 4.3% a year. Nothing here names a protocol: every number comes from the protocol's own record.
